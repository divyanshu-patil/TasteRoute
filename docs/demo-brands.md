# Demo brands

Real Brooklyn food and drink brands for testing and the demo video. Each site was read with the app's own reader (up to 3 pages) and each name was looked up with Qloo `/search` on 2026-10-19. Sites change, so re-check one before recording.

Paste the website into step 1. If the site can't be read, use "Describe instead" and paste the description.

## Descriptions to paste

**St. Agrestis**
> We make non-alcoholic bottled cocktails in Greenpoint, Brooklyn: the Phony Negroni and five variations, including an espresso version with caffeine and a booze-free amaro. Ready to drink, 30 botanicals.

**Kings County Distillery**
> We're a Brooklyn whiskey distillery, founded in 2010 in the Brooklyn Navy Yard. We make handmade bourbon, rye and coffee whiskey.

**Partners Coffee**
> We roast coffee daily in Brooklyn: easy-drinking blends and rotating single origins, sourced through long-term farm partnerships.

**SEY**
> We're a small Brooklyn roastery focused on light, seasonal, complex coffees, with close relationships across the supply chain.

**Raaka Chocolate**
> We make organic, vegan chocolate from unroasted cacao, traded transparently, in Brooklyn. Bars and minis.

**Devoción**
> We roast farm-fresh Colombian specialty coffee. Our team in Colombia sources from farms all year, so the beans arrive fresh.

## Suggested demo order

1. St. Agrestis: Qloo knows the brand itself, so step 3 shows "Qloo knows this".
2. One coffee brand (SEY or Partners): shows the similar-brands path in step 3.
3. Kings County Distillery (optional): a spirits brand gets a bar-heavy list.
