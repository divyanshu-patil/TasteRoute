# Stockist backtest: does TasteRoute find a brand's real accounts?

Generated by `apps/api/scripts/evaluate_stockists.py` on 2026-10-20. Method pre-registered in `docs/15-evaluation.md` ("Stockist backtest") before any test brand was run. Ground truth: `docs/eval/stockists.csv`; pools: `docs/eval/pools/`.

Each brand's Brooklyn accounts come from the brand's own public account list. Every method ranks the same pool: Qloo's 500 best-known Brooklyn venues of each of the brand's venue types (popularity order, no taste signal). An account counts when it is in the pool, so accounts and other venues are picked the same way. No method sees the brand's name.

| Method | How it ranks the pool |
|---|---|
| **Q-fit** (TasteRoute) | The app's fit: Qloo taste for the comparables vs mass-market taste at the same venues |
| Q-raw | Qloo affinity for the comparables only |
| POP | Qloo popularity only |
| LLM | Gemma 4 31B from the description, the comparables and each venue's name, type and neighbourhood |
| Q-fit+LLM | Exploratory (added after the dev run, before the test run): the mean of the Q-fit and LLM positions |
| Q-cult | Qloo affinity with a music signal: the 5 artists the comparables' fans over-index on (added after the test run, pre-registered before it ran) |
| Q-fit+C | The mean of the Q-fit and Q-cult positions; enters the app's fit only under the pre-registered rule |
| Q-heat | Qloo heatmap lift at the venue: comparables minus mass-market affinity in the nearest heatmap cell, as the app's taste route (added after the music check, pre-registered before it ran) |
| Q-fit+H | The mean of the Q-fit and Q-heat positions; judged by the pre-registered rule |
| RANDOM | Expected value |

**Result: Q-fit beat the LLM on recall@30 in 1 of 3 scored test brands (1 tied). Target was 3 of 4: missed.**

Not scored: **Steven Graf Wine** (test): 0 of 6 comparables (Gut Oggau, Martha Stoumen, Broc Cellars, Kermit Lynch Wine Merchant, Scribe Winery, Donkey & Goat) are known to Qloo; the minimum is 3.

## Where Qloo lost and why

Written after the test run; every number is in the tables above or in `docs/eval/results-all.json`.

- **Qloo doesn't know the natural-wine world (Steven Graf Wine).** None of six well-known natural-wine producers and importers resolve in Qloo `/search`, so there is no taste signal. The app fails closed in that case (step 3 says Qloo doesn't know the brands), and the pre-registered rule did the same here. The script only enforced that rule after the first test run, which had ranked this brand with no signal (recall@30 Q-fit 0%, LLM 20%; kept in `docs/eval/results-test.json`).
- **The mass-market baseline works against coffee (Parlor Coffee).** The two café accounts, Odd Fox Coffee and Petee's Café, sit at Qloo taste percentiles 96 and 90 but also at mass-market percentiles 87 and 83, because the mass-market brands include Starbucks and Dunkin'. Lift pushes them down and Q-fit's top 10 turns into restaurants and hotels. Q-raw's top 10 is specialty cafés (SEY, Blue Bottle, Black Brick) and finds 1 of the 4 accounts, as the LLM does. Only 4 of 47 accounts are among Qloo's best-known venues, so this brand says little either way.
- **Lift did not help on this test.** Q-raw matched or beat Q-fit on recall@30 for every scored test brand (Parlor 25% vs 0%, St. Agrestis 22% vs 19%, Casamara Club 14% vs 14%).
- **The LLM reads names and knows famous venues.** Its mean percentile rank beats Q-fit on all three scored test brands (86 vs 78, 73 vs 66, 70 vs 69) and both dev brands. Its top picks carry the category in the name ("Coffee Project", "The Whiskey Annex", "Black Mountain Wine House") or are famous (Lilia, The Four Horsemen).
- **Where Qloo was ahead: the top of the list for taste-led brands.** St. Agrestis recall@15: Q-raw 19% (7 of 36), Q-fit 11%, LLM 11%. Accounts Qloo ranks high and the LLM does not: Grand Army (Q-fit 9th, LLM 284th), June (2nd vs 101st), Madeline's (22nd vs 283rd), all driven by fans of Ghia and Campari. For Casamara Club the LLM has no account in its top 30; Q-fit has Glasserie at 10th.
- **Music taste did not help ranking (checked under a rule written before it ran).** With the 5 artists the comparables' fans over-index on as the signal, Q-fit+C put 4 test accounts in its top 30 against Q-fit's 8, and 1 Kings County Distillery account against 2, so cultural fit stays an explanation and does not change fit. The artists are national tastes (country singers for both whiskey and rosé fans), which say little about which Brooklyn bar a brand belongs in. Q-cult alone put 2 of 7 Wölffer Estate accounts in its top 30, where every other method found none; one dev brand is not evidence.

- **The taste heatmap did not help ranking either (checked under a rule written before it ran).** Q-fit+H averages Q-fit with Q-heat (Qloo heatmap lift at each venue's location). It put 6 test accounts in its top 30, against 8 for Q-fit and 8 for the LLM, and none of Kings County Distillery's against Q-fit's 2. So the heatmap stays a route and a map layer and never changes fit. Q-heat alone had the best mean percentile rank for Casamara Club (85; LLM 70, Q-fit 69): The Hop Shop is 25th by heatmap, 203rd by Q-fit and 592nd for the LLM. One brand is not evidence. Every venue near the same heatmap cell gets the same lift, so the heatmap says which streets to walk, not which door to knock on.

**What it means.** On these brands Qloo's taste signal finds about as many real accounts as an LLM that reads venue names: 8 of 47 in the top 30 for Q-fit, 8 for the LLM, 10 for Q-raw, about 1.5 by chance. They find different ones (Grand Army and June for Qloo, Lilia and Cecconi's for the LLM), so the app shows both (the LLM column on the results page) instead of claiming Qloo wins. The mass-market set should not contain the brand's own category (no coffee chains for a coffee brand); that change needs new brands to test it on, so it is logged, not made.

## Limits

- One city and six brands; the four test brands decide the claim. Small samples: one account moves recall@30 by several points.
- Account lists are what each brand publishes; some are out of date, and shops drop out at resolution.
- Accounts outside Qloo's 500 best-known venues per type are excluded for every method, so the test says nothing about the least-known venues.
- A venue Qloo returns no taste reading for counts as the lowest taste score.
- The LLM scores the pool in batches of 50, so its scores are only compared within the pool order it was given (fixed hash).
